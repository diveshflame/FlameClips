## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-precision, focused editing environment tailored for short-form video creators, agencies, and social media strategists. The interface prioritizes deep immersion, cognitive endurance, and rapid decision-making during asset selection and timeline arrangement.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Elevated Dark Minimalist with disciplined cybernetic touches. Interfaces are anchored in near-black charcoal surfaces that prioritize high-framerate visual content.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Visual energy stems from restrained electric violet and indigo gradients, reserved for computational highlights, virality score badges, and primary generative actions.
- **Subtle Surface Definition:** High-definition structural clarity is achieved using low-opacity perimeter strokes, fine 1px inner highlights, and soft localized glow filters rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Tone:** Autonomous, pro-grade, razor-sharp, and distraction-free.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for a dark-first production workflow to reduce eye fatigue during long video review sessions.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#6366F1`):** Electric Indigo. Serves as the interactive anchor for generation triggers, timeline cursors, active navigation markers, and AI intelligence indicators.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Emerald. Strictly designated for high-virality ratings (85+), rendering success states, and live output badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Amber. Dedicated to asynchronous queuing, video transcoding notices, audio clipping warnings, and token usage warnings.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F19`):** Deep Obsidian. Serves as the root canvas.

### Color Distribution & Application
- **Canvas Base:** `#0B0F19` provides absolute contrast against 9:16 vertical video thumbnails.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Shell):** `#111827` at 90% opacity over canvas.
- **Surface Level 2 (Cards & Active Groups):** `#161F36` with a border stroke of `#334155` at 50% opacity.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns):** `#1E293B`.
- **Primary AI Gradient:** Linear 135deg from `#4F46E5` to `#818CF8`. Use exclusively on batch export triggers, primary generation actions, and AI score pills.
- **Borders:** Restrict neutral borders to 1px solid `#1E293B` or `#334155` with alpha channels between 0.35 and 0.6. Avoid solid bright lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances modern SaaS readability with dense data visibility:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric curves and generous counters provide a contemporary brand voice without competing against video previews.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** High x-height, clear optical kerning, and neutral letterforms guarantee effortless reading across captions, metadata panels, and script previews.
- **Technical & Metric Data (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for video timecodes (`00:14:22`), virality probability metrics (`Score: 94.8`), export resolutions, aspect ratios (`9:16`), and token meters.

### Formatting Rules
- Keep headings above 24px in pure white (`#F8FAFC`).
- Body text defaults to neutral slate (`#94A3B8`). Muted labels and secondary explanations drop to `#64748B`.
- Numeric values must enable tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter during live timeline playback and dynamic scoring tickers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for complex studio workspace layouts, prioritizing high information density alongside spatial breathing room.

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (1440px+):** Fixed 260px collapsible primary navigation sidebar, dynamic multi-column workbench containing an active 9:16 preview canvas (center) flanked by generation panels (380px) and timeline clip feeds.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** Sidebar retracts into a slim 64px icon rail. Center player and side inspect panes collapse into structured vertical tabs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Timeline actions convert into fixed bottom-sheet drawers; the video view anchors at the top with auto-calculated height.

### Spatial Discipline
- Base internal spacing follows an uncompromising 4px/8px modular rhythm.
- Dense operational panels (timeline tracks, clip trimming handles) leverage `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Card containers and view headers preserve clarity using `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid murky visuals in dark mode, elevation relies on luminance layering, edge highlights, and calibrated optical glows rather than dark drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Surface 0 (Background Canvas):** Solid `#0B0F19`. Zero elevation.
2. **Surface 1 (Panels & Toolbars):** `#111827` at 95% opacity with backdrop blur (12px). Encased with a 1px top border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` and side/bottom borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
3. **Surface 2 (Interactive Cards & Timeline Chunks):** `#161F36` at 85% opacity. Surrounded by a 1px border of `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)` when inactive, and `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.5)` when focused.
4. **Surface 3 (Floating Overlays, Select Menus, Tooltips):** `#1E293B` with a diffuse ambient shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.

### Optical Glow Filters
Active items, processing clips, and top AI score nodes receive a soft, color-tinted back-glow:
- **Violet Glow:** `0 0 24px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`
- **Success Glow:** `0 0 20px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp, technical utility with comfortable tactile contours:

- **Corner Radii:** Structural default is `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Cards & Video Shells (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Framing for generated video outputs, media bin cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Controls & Buttons (`rounded` / 8px):** Precision inputs, timeline scrubber handles, action triggers, and segmented buttons.
- **Tags, Chips, & Badges (`rounded-full`):** Virality metrics, timestamp counters, and processing status markers use full capsule curvature to differentiate metadata from interactive cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Generate Clips", "Export All"):** Linear gradient background (`#4F46E5` to `#6366F1`), high-contrast pure white text, 8px radius, subtle top-edge inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Hover expands a violet ambient glow (`0 0 20px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Surface background `#1E293B`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Hover shifts background to `#25334D` with text turning from slate-300 to slate-100.
- **Ghost / Icon Actions:** Transparent background, slate-400 icons. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with icon tint moving to `#818CF8`.

### Chips & Badges
- **AI Virality Score Pill:** Capsule shape, `JetBrains Mono` bold 11px. High-tier (85-100) utilizes a 10% emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), `#10B981` text, and a fine 1px border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`. Mid-tier uses amber tokens; low-tier uses muted slate.
- **Social Destination Chips (TikTok, Shorts, Reels):** Dark neutral background with an icon prefix and active state toggled via a thin electric violet boundary ring.

### Cards (Clip Card Variant)
- **Structure:** Aspect ratio preserved thumbnail area followed by metadata and quick-actions below.
- **Styling:** `#131B2E` background, 16px radius, encased with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **States:** Hover smoothly lifts border illumination to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)` and applies an internal overlay gradient on the video preview. Selected clip adds an active 2px outer border of `#6366F1`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** `#0E1424` recessed interior with 8px radius.
- **Border:** 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#6366F1` with an outer ring spread of `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#475569`.
- **Prepend/Append Slots:** Monospace timecode badges, URL scraper prompts, and Lucide search icons set to `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px, 4px radius. Checked state fills with `#6366F1` displaying a crisp white vector checkmark. Unchecked state shows `#0E1424` with a 1px border of `#334155`.
- **Toggles / Switches:** Capsule frame with 20px height, 36px width. Off-state `#1E293B`, On-state gradient violet. Thumb is 16px crisp white with smooth horizontal spring transition.

### Custom Creator Components
- **Timeline Trimmer Bar:** Dual-ended range slider in electric indigo with active frame preview strip, frame-accurate monospace indicators, and vertical playhead line in `#FFFFFF`.
- **Transcript Synchronizer Box:** Highlighting active spoken word chunks in real-time with violet underline accents and editable caption text blocks.
